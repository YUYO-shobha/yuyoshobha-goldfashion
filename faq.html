Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find answers to common questions about ordering, payment, shipping, and support. Currently, all orders are placed through WhatsApp for quick and easy service.

1. How do I place an order?

You can select your product from our website and click on the “Order on WhatsApp” button. You will be redirected to WhatsApp where you can confirm your order with us.

2. Do I need to create an account?

No account is required. You can directly order through WhatsApp.

3. How do I confirm my order?

Once you message us on WhatsApp, our team will confirm product availability, price, and delivery details.

4. What payment methods are available?

You can pay via UPI, bank transfer, or Cash on Delivery (if available for your location).

5. How long does delivery take?

Delivery usually takes 3–7 working days depending on your location.

6. Do you offer free shipping?

Yes, we offer free shipping on selected orders or above a minimum purchase value (as applicable).

7. Can I cancel or return my order?

Orders can be cancelled before dispatch. Returns are accepted only if the product is damaged or as per our return policy.

8. How can I contact support?

You can contact us anytime through WhatsApp or the Contact page on our website.
